Battery Safety Information

Chemistry: Li-ion (LiCoO2/Graphite)
Harmful substances other than Pb, Cd and Hg: LiPF6 electrolyte; flammable organic carbonate solvents (EC, EMC, DEC, PC)
Proportion of key raw materials: LiCoO2 47%, Graphite 20.2%, Cu 7.7%, Ni 6.8%, Al 4.6%, LiPF6 1.35%
Usable extinguishing agent: Water, dry sand
EU Declaration of Conformity:
Waste battery management information:
  • Do not dispose of with household waste; take to a designated collection point
  • Contains lithium — do not disassemble, puncture, crush, heat, or incinerate
  • Meaning of label symbols: crossed-out bin = separate collection required; CE = EU conformity marking
  • Hazardous substances in this battery (LiPF6 electrolyte, cobalt oxide) can harm the environment and human health if improperly discarded — always recycle through designated channels
